Hi! Thanks for ordering a car-hacking kit from us! Our documentation for this product is still under heavy revision, so check back frequently. If there's a hole in the documentation that you're interested in filling, we welcome contributions! We're still learning, so we would appreciate any feedback you have: feel free to email us at \href{mailto:info@electroniqbuttons.com}{info@electroniqbuttons.com}. It's also important to us that everything works. Please email us at \href{mailto:support@electroniqbuttons.com}{support@electroniqbuttons.com} if that's not the case. 

Included in the car-hacking kit:
{\small
\begin{enumerate}
  \item (if selected) 1 WiCAN-OBD-C3 flashed with the latest beta firmware
  \item 1 custom harness for the central CAN hub (ICU or CCU)
  \item Mounting supplies: 
    \begin{enumerate}
      \item 1 60mm OBD mount bracket
      \item 2 pieces of double-sided adhesive, cut to 60mm (1 extra in case of mishaps; email us if you need more)
      \item 4 zip ties
    \end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}
}
